fix: bridge connection actually happens, retries, and finds node

Three causes stacked:
1. after the default UI switched to the workbench, nothing called agent:connect
   at all - the old panel connected inside a:open(), the workbench only painted
   the status. So the default UI never even attempted a connection.
2. Agent:connect connected once and gave up; the bridge takes a second or two to
   listen after startBridge, so the first attempt was always refused.
3. startBridge used bare "node" and never checked whether a bridge was already
   listening.

now: connectIfNeeded() starts the chain for the workbench; connect() runs a
1s x 6 backoff with single-flight scheduling, ignored late callbacks from old
connections and a readable conclusion when exhausted; resolveNode tries PATH,
ProgramFiles and LOCALAPPDATA; portInUse skips starting a second bridge; the
workbench retry button calls the real connect and the status line follows real
callbacks.
